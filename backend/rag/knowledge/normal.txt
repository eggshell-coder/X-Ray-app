[Normal]

Key sign:
- No significant opacities, consolidation, or effusion
- Cardiothoracic ratio within normal range (< 0.5)
- Clear costophrenic angles
- Normal lung markings without fibrotic or nodular pattern

Region to inspect:
- Whole-lung survey (no single localized region) — specifically confirm
  cardiac silhouette size and both costophrenic angles are unremarkable

Why this matters:
A "Normal" prediction is a negative finding — it means the model did not
detect the sign patterns associated with TB, Cardiac Pathology, Pleural
Pathology, or Chronic Lung Disease. It is not a confirmation of health, only
an absence of the specific patterns the model was trained to recognize.

Model reliability note:
This is a class where a false "Normal" prediction carries real clinical risk.
On the pooled cross-validation set (support=171), when the model predicts
Normal there remains roughly a 12.1% residual probability that the case is
actually pathological (NPV = 0.879, 95% CI 0.840-0.909) — most often an
early-stage or borderline Cardiac Pathology case being read as Normal. This
motivates treating Normal predictions, especially at moderate confidence,
with the same review threshold as other classes rather than assuming an
automatic pass. On the smaller held-out test set (n=57, support=19), the
most frequent confusions were: misread as Cardiac (3 cases) or, less often,
Chronic Lung Disease (1 case) — no Normal case was misclassified as TB or
Pleural, meaning the model's errors on this class are conservative rather
than catastrophic.
