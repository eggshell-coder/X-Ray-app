[TB - Tuberculosis]

Key sign:
- Nodular consolidation and cavitation
- Upper-lobe infiltrates
- Fibrotic scarring in apical region
- Hilar/mediastinal lymphadenopathy
- Miliary pattern (small scattered nodules) in advanced/disseminated cases

Region to inspect:
- Upper lung zones (apical/posterior segments)
- Hilar region

Why this region:
TB bacteria thrive in high-oxygen environments, which upper lung lobes provide.
This is why apical predominance is considered a classic radiological marker of TB.
